# S12 humid air and phase exchange

The closed gas diffusion step applies a binary mass-fraction gradient across gas-open cut-cell faces. It exchanges equal and opposite water-vapour and dry-air mass and transports the corresponding species enthalpy. The caller supplies gas-open area, group gas mass, component enthalpies and the binary diffusivity. An explicit timestep guard prevents overdraw. The two-cell CPU check recovered 8.910599e-10 kg vapour transfer and conserved total enthalpy.

The low Mach helpers derive the projection source from the fixed-mass change in EOS gas volume and derive buoyancy relative to the ambient hydrostatic pressure gradient. The CPU checks produced upward accelerations of 0.16780046 m/s² for a 5 K warming and 0.029469955 m/s² for an increase in water mass fraction from 0.005 to 0.01.

The planar film solve uses the S02 chemical-equilibrium composition and a Stefan correction for zero net dry-air flux. It balances conductive heat from the liquid and gas against latent heat at the interface. The dilute approximation uses pure saturated vapour enthalpy and prescribed gas-film thickness and thermal conductances. The CPU fixtures returned 292.931469 K for an evaporating interface between 293.15 K reservoirs and 283.370361 K for a condensing interface between 283.15 K reservoirs. The integrated inventory source closed liquid and vapour mass, enthalpy, and phase-change volume. At matched equilibrium composition, the isothermal flux was zero.

The VOF transport reconstructs the gas side of mixed cells and clips both phase planes on each shared face. The shared gas aperture is bounded by the embedded solid aperture and the two one-sided solid sections. The CPU fixture recovered 5e-5 m² on a half-wetted face and 4e-5 m² when a fixed slab blocked one fifth of that opening. The corresponding vapour transfers matched the analytical gradients.

Open gas faces accept a prescribed reservoir state and use the cell-to-boundary distance for the diffusion gradient. The boundary ledger records signed vapour and enthalpy exchange. CPU fixtures passed for both inward and outward vapour flux, with equal dry-air counterflow. This is diffusive exchange at fixed gas mass. Advective backflow remains part of the flow transport.

The phase-exchange transport step supplies the negative net phase volume rate to the pressure projection and reconciles the accepted face flux against the same volume increment. It advects the original inventory and then applies the signed liquid, gas, enthalpy and vapour increments. Open CPU fixtures checked both evaporation outflow and condensation inflow, reconciling every transported inventory with the boundary ledger. This path takes group sources as inputs. Reconstructing the source on a moving interface and V27–V28 benchmark comparisons remain open.

Run the reference executable with `FlowMain --humid-diffusion`. In Unity, **Physics Sim → Three Phase 3D → Run S12 GPU phase exchange check** uses the shared fixture with GPU pressure projection and CPU phase transport. The user-run check at commit 4dd41675550aca1184e55c079746edfaa46c98b6 reached its final pass message after both projected cases. It reported 292.931469 K for evaporation and 283.370361 K for condensation.

The reconstructed exchange step clips the PLIC interface inside each mixed cell, subtracts the solid cross-section and sums the exposed area by pressure group. It uses that area to convert the supplied interfacial solution into group sources. CPU checks recovered the expected planar area with and without an embedded slab. In the projected fixture, evaporation generated 8.1865751e-11 m³ of expansion and condensation generated 7.8802682e-11 m³ of contraction. The open boundary compensated each to within tolerance. This implementation uses mixed-cell interfaces, so a face-aligned interface between pure cells still requires an explicit treatment. Temperature and composition inputs for the local interfacial solve remain caller-supplied. The updated reconstructed-area fixture has CPU evidence.
